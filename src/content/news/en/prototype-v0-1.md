Mirrowick v0.1 is out, and you can [play it in the browser](https://play.mirrowick.com/) for free.

It is an early prototype of Chapter I, _The Fading Tomorrow_. Here is what is in it:

- the intro and a few cutscenes, starting with the call from your uncle’s neighbour;
- Victor’s homestead, with the shed and the cellar under it;
- the Facility: its underground level −1 and the yard of level 0 above it;
- light and shadow, doors, notes and letters to read, and an inventory;
- a real-time fight with a few foes, running and tiring, and autosave;
- survival mode, which opens once you reach the end of the demo.

The game is in English and Russian.

We will write here about what changes and how it is made.
